ORGANIZATIONAL HUB

About HRD Forum Nepal

A united national coalition defending human rights activists, coordinating legal protection, and strengthening grassroots watchdogs across all 7 provinces.

Constitutional Mandate

Empowered by international declarations and Nepal's Constitution, we monitor civic space violations, secure rule of law, and protect human rights defenders from judicial harassment and unlawful detention.

Mission Statement

To establish a rapid protective ecosystem across all 7 provinces that safeguards local activists, provides 24/7 legal aid, and ensures human rights defenders operate without fear of reprisal.

Vision Statement

A democratic Nepal where all grassroots advocates, journalists, and civic watchdogs enjoy full constitutional liberties and institutional security under absolute rule of law.
