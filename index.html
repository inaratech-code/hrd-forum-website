ORGANIZATIONAL BACKGROUND

About HRD Forum Nepal

Human Rights Defenders Forum (HRD Forum) Nepal functions as a united national NGO committed to creating a secure and empowering environment for civic activists. Operating under constitutional mandates and universal declarations, we monitor safety, investigate violations, and empower vulnerable actors.

Through our strategic presence across all seven provinces of Nepal, we bridge critical gaps between local defenders and international protective structures. We believe that securing the rights of defenders is the first step toward safeguarding democratic freedom for all Nepalese citizens.
