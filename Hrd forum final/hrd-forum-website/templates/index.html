About HRD Forum Nepal

Human Rights Defenders (HRD) Forum Nepal is a national coalition dedicated to protecting, empowering, and advocating for frontline rights defenders, journalists, paralegals, and civil society activists across Nepal. Established to respond to shrinking civic space and increasing security threats, the Forum operates as a unified shield for those facing retaliation, harassment, or legal intimidation while defending fundamental human rights.

Through localized emergency assistance, continuous field monitoring, strategic legal aid, and evidence-based policy advocacy, HRD Forum Nepal bridges grassroots rights defenders with national judicial frameworks and international protection mechanisms. Our network ensures that no defender stands alone in their pursuit of justice and constitutional freedoms across all seven provinces.

Human Rights Advocacy

Lobbying for constitutional freedoms, civic space preservation, and systemic human rights reforms across provincial and national governance levels.

Defender Protection

Providing rapid emergency response, legal defense funds, temporary relocation, and 24/7 helpline intervention for defenders under threat.

Policy & Research

Publishing empirical field reports, security situation assessments, and policy documentation to inform legislative and judicial bodies.
