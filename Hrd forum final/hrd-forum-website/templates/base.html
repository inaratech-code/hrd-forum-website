About HRD Forum Nepal

Organization Mandate & Mission

Human Rights Defenders Forum Nepal is a national alliance of frontline activists, constitutional lawyers, and civic watchdogs working across all 7 provinces of Nepal. We monitor security threats, log emergency incidents, conduct high-level judicial advocacy, and operate 24/7 localized helpdesks to protect defenders facing threats or detention.
